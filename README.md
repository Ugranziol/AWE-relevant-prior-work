# AWE — Relevant Prior Work

## Adaptive and Longitudinal Framework for Evaluating AI-Related User Wellbeing

This page provides selected prior work demonstrating the complementary expertise of the AWE research team in mathematical psychology, psychometrics, adaptive psychological assessment, clinical psychology, artificial intelligence, and large language model evaluation.

The selected publications are particularly relevant to the methodological foundations of AWE, including formal representations of multidimensional psychological states, computerized adaptive assessment, psychometric and clinical validation, and the systematic evaluation of artificial intelligence systems.

---

## Umberto Granziol
### Formal and Adaptive Psychological Assessment

Granziol's prior work includes the development and empirical validation of formal and computerized adaptive methods for psychological assessment. This work provides a methodological foundation for AWE's use of structured psychological state spaces and adaptive navigation.

Granziol, U., Brancaccio, A., Pizziconi, G., Spangaro, M., Gentili, F., Bosia, M., Gregori, E., Luperini, C., Pavan, C., Santarelli, V., Cavallaro, R., Cremonese, C., Favaro, A., Rossi, A., Vidotto, G., & Spoto, A. (2022). On the implementation of computerized adaptive observations for psychological assessment. *Assessment, 29*(2), 225–241.  
https://doi.org/10.1177/1073191120960215

Granziol, U., Spoto, A., & Vidotto, G. (2018). The assessment of nonverbal behavior in schizophrenia through the Formal Psychological Assessment. *International Journal of Methods in Psychiatric Research, 27*(1), e1595.  
https://doi.org/10.1002/mpr.1595

Donadello, I., Spoto, A., Sambo, F., Badaloni, S., Granziol, U., & Vidotto, G. (2017). ATS-PD: An adaptive testing system for psychological disorders. *Educational and Psychological Measurement, 77*(5), 792–815.  
https://doi.org/10.1177/0013164416652188

## Andrea Spoto

Spoto's work provides the mathematical and psychometric foundations for representing psychological constructs as structured systems of attributes and for using these structures in computerized adaptive assessment.

https://doi.org/10.3758/BRM.42.1.342

Serra, F., Spoto, A., Ghisi, M., & Vidotto, G. (2015). Formal Psychological Assessment in evaluating depression: A new methodology to build exhaustive and irredundant adaptive questionnaires. *PLOS ONE, 10*(4), e0122131.

## Alberto Testolin
### Artificial Intelligence and LLM Evaluation

Testolin's research contributes expertise in artificial intelligence, neural and neuro-symbolic systems, large language models, and systematic model evaluation. This expertise supports the AI-facing component of AWE and the use of modern AI capabilities within formally constrained evaluation structures.

Petruzzellis, F., Testolin, A., & Sperduti, A. (2024). Benchmarking GPT-4 on algorithmic problems: A systematic evaluation of prompting strategies. In *Proceedings of the 2024 Joint International Conference on Computational Linguistics, Language Resources and Evaluation (LREC-COLING 2024)*.  
https://aclanthology.org/2024.lrec-main.195/

Petruzzellis, F., Testolin, A., & Sperduti, A. (2026). Learning neuro-symbolic convergent term rewriting systems. *Artificial Intelligence*, 104622.

## Gioia Bottesi
### Clinical Psychology and Multidimensional Psychological Assessment

Bottesi's work contributes the clinical and psychological assessment expertise required to define and validate wellbeing-relevant constructs. Her research includes formal and multidimensional approaches that move beyond aggregate psychological scores to characterize clinically meaningful configurations of psychological attributes.

Bottesi, G., Spoto, A., Freeston, M. H., Sanavio, E., & Vidotto, G. (2015). Beyond the score: Clinical evaluation through formal psychological assessment. *Journal of Personality Assessment, 97*(3), 252–260.  
https://doi.org/10.1080/00223891.2014.958846

Bottesi, G., Marchetti, I., Sica, C., & Ghisi, M. (2020). What is the internal structure of intolerance of uncertainty? A network analysis approach. *Journal of Anxiety Disorders, 75*, 102293.  
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.janxdis.2020.102293

## Relevance to AWE

Together, these publications demonstrate prior expertise across the core methodological components required by AWE:

- formal representation of multidimensional psychological states;
- Knowledge Space Theory and Formal Psychological Assessment;
- computerized and adaptive psychological assessment;
- psychometric and clinical validation;
- multidimensional approaches to psychological constructs;
- artificial intelligence and neuro-symbolic methods; and
- systematic evaluation of large language models.

AWE builds on these established lines of research to develop an adaptive and longitudinal framework for evaluating wellbeing-relevant behavior in human–AI interaction.
